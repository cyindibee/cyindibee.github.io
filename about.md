# About the project

CyIndiBee develops cyberinfrastructure for the video analysis of individual honey bee behavior. Cameras at hive entrances and flowers record thousands of bees; our tools detect, track and re-identify individual bees, whether they carry a paint mark or a numbered tag, and recognize events such as entering or leaving the hive and carrying pollen.

The goal is to give biologists reliable, large-scale measurements of what each bee does over time, so they can study questions such as foraging, circadian rhythms and the effects of environmental factors on colonies.

## Approach

- **Detection and tracking** of bees in video recorded at hive entrances and flower patches.
- **Re-identification** of individual bees from paint marks and number tags.
- **Behavior and event recognition**, such as entrances, exits and pollen foraging.
- **Edge computing** systems to process video in the field in real time.
